# The Content Slate Rule

<!-- For agents that make things for the public: posts, videos, essays, graphics. -->
<!-- Every piece can be good on its own and the slate can still be a rut. Check the slate, not just the piece. -->

## The rule

Before proposing or making anything new:

1. **Look at the last 5 outputs** across every platform, not just this one.
2. **Note four things for each:** topic, dataset or source, format, angle.
3. **No topic repeats within 3 outputs.** A topic may return only with a genuinely new cut — a new question, a new slice of the data, a new story.
4. **Vary the shape too.** Same opener, same structure, same closing line twice in a row reads as machine-made. It fails review even if the facts are perfect.
5. **Run the usefulness test.** Before anything ships, it must answer three questions:
   - Who does this help?
   - What can they do with it that they could not do before?
   - What is the one true thing it teaches?

   A piece that cannot answer is decoration. Decoration does not ship.

## Why

Quality gates check a single piece: numbers right, visuals clean, wording safe.
Nobody was checking the whole slate — until the user noticed five good pieces in a row
were all about the same topic. Variety is part of the quality bar. It is the agent's job
to watch it, never the user's job to request it.
